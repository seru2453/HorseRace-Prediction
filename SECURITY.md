# SECURITY

- Do not store secrets in Git.
- Do not allow arbitrary user-provided URLs.
- JRA live fetches are domain-allowlisted.
- Internal stack traces are not shown in the browser UI.
- SQLAlchemy is used instead of hand-built SQL strings.
- The app does not buy tickets, connect to voting sites, calculate stakes, or guarantee profit.
- JRA copyrighted article text, images, and video are not bundled or redistributed.
